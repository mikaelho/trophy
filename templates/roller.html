Roll Reference

Hunt Roll
Explore your environment
  • 1 light die for exploring and asking the GM questions.
  • +1 light die if a Skill or Equipment eases the hunt.
  • Read the highest die:
    • 1 — lose all Hunt Tokens; something terrible.
    • 2–3 — something terrible.
    • 4–5 — gain 1 Hunt Token, and something terrible.
    • 6 — gain 1 Hunt Token.
  • The GM answers your questions regardless of the roll.
Risk Roll
Before trying something risky
  • Ask the GM and players what could go wrong.
  • 1 light die if skilled (Occupation/Background) or with useful Equipment.
  • +1 light die for accepting a Devil's Bargain.
  • 1 dark die if risking mind or body (required for Powers).
  • Did a dark die meet or beat the highest light die?
    • Yes — if that dark die beats your Ruin, mark 1 Ruin.
    • No — you may risk mind/body to re-roll with +1 dark die.
  • Read the highest die: 1–3 fail · 4–5 success with a complication · 6 success.
Combat Roll
Defeat a monster together
  • Decide who participates.
  • Each participant says how they're vulnerable and rolls 1 light die — their Weak Point.
  • 1 dark die per attacker with a usable weapon.
  • Did any dark die match a Weak Point?
    • Mark 1 Ruin per match, or mark a piece of Armor to avoid all Ruin.
  • Do the two highest dark dice total the monster's Endurance or more?
    • Yes — success, the monster is defeated.
    • No — risk more: +1 dark die and re-roll.
  • Harvest: roll dice equal to the monster's Endurance; each 6 scavenges 1 Coin.
Contest Roll
Scavengers against each other
  • Agree on what is at stake.
  • Each player: 1 light die if a Skill or Equipment helps.
  • +1 light die per mark of Ruin you currently have.
  • 1 dark die if the contest is deadly — take as many as you dare.
  • Most 6s wins (ties: most 5s, then 4s, and so on).
  • For each dark die showing a 1, mark 1 Ruin.
Help Roll
Aid another player's Risk Roll
  • You may help when their Risk Roll includes at least one dark die.
  • Say how you expose yourself to danger, then roll 1 light die.
  • They may use your light die in their roll.
  • Does your light die match any dark die in their roll?
    • Yes — mark 1 Ruin.
